Frequently asked questions

How much does Tresqu cost?

For now, Tresqu is completely free. All you need is WhatsApp, Telegram, or a web browser.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Tresqu lives in the chat: text it on WhatsApp or Telegram, or use it on the web. The dashboard opens in your browser.

Does Tresqu handle my money?

No. Your money lives in Wallbit. Tresqu only proposes actions that you confirm, with limits you define.

Is my Wallbit API key safe?

Yes. We encrypt it with AES-128 on our server. It never reaches your browser or shows up in logs, and you can revoke it whenever you want.

How do I connect my Wallbit account?

Go to your Tresqu dashboard, open your profile, and paste your API key. It takes a minute. If you don't have a Wallbit account yet, open one and then connect it.
