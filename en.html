Knows how you live. Invests like you.

Tresqu is a team of financial agents you talk to through chat (WhatsApp, Telegram, Gmail, or the web). It logs your expenses, understands your income, and uses all that context to invest with you on Wallbit.

The agent team

  • Expenses & income: logs and categorizes every transaction, builds monthly summaries, and finds patterns in your history.
  • Investments (Wallbit): prepares buys, sells, and fund transfers. It never executes without your confirmation.
  • Market analyst: brings the price, performance, and fundamentals of a stock or ETF and crosses them with your risk profile and portfolio. Context, not advice.
  • Risk profile: measures your real tolerance from your history — not a questionnaire — and holds back purchases that don't fit you.

How you log

  • Text, voice, or photo: write the way you talk, send a voice note or a photo of the receipt, and Tresqu understands, extracts, and categorizes it instantly.
  • Automatic Gmail: connect your inbox and it detects purchases in your emails without you typing anything.
  • Search by meaning: ask in natural language, no filters or exact categories needed.

Investing with Wallbit

Your money lives in Wallbit, not in Tresqu. From the chat you can buy and sell stocks, ETFs, and bonds, contribute to your Chests, move funds, activate or pause your card, and check balances and positions live. Tresqu only proposes operations that you confirm, with limits you define. API keys are encrypted with Fernet encryption and never reach the client.

Multi-currency: COP, USD, EUR, and other major currencies, with live conversions.
